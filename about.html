From UI/UX Designer & Auditor To AI Consultant

The Struggle

I started my journey in UI/UX design, building out mobile apps and websites. Eventually, I thought the future was in AR/VR and spent over a year mapping out my career in that direction.

At the same time, I was working my day job as an IT systems auditor for a consulting firm. Sitting down with clients, examining their pain points, and auditing their security controls, a very obvious pattern emerged:

  • Most businesses are drowning in manual, repetitive workflows.
  • Most businesses are wasting hours manually searching through spreadsheets and data.
  • Most businesses don't just need a shiny new app; they need to stop losing time to inefficient processes.

The Shift

Then the tech landscape shifted. Big tech shelved their AR/VR headsets and went all-in on AI agents.

The real turning point for me happened in October. While working with a major enterprise client, I gained access to Microsoft Copilot. Suddenly, the tasks that used to require me to manually dig through spreadsheets for business risks and security controls were being done for me — in half the time, and with better accuracy.

That's when it clicked.

If enterprise companies were using this to completely eliminate repetitive admin work, it was only a matter of time before these systems trickled down to small businesses. Just building a beautiful interface wasn't enough if the business operations behind it were still stuck in the past.

The Solution

I stopped acting just as a designer asking, "What do you want me to build?" Instead, I adopted the consultant's mindset. I started asking the questions that actually move the needle for a business:

  • Where are you losing time?
  • Where are you losing money?

Now, I combine my background in web and mobile app design with advanced AI automation (like Vision 6 systems) to completely overhaul small business operations.

My process is straightforward:

01

Audit

Identify the manual bottlenecks and dynamics slowing the business down.

02

Build

Replace manual, repetitive tasks with structured systems powered by AI.

03

Iterate

Test, refine, and improve the systems based on real usage and data.

The goal isn't just to make things look better. The goal is to bring enterprise-level AI efficiency to small businesses so they can save time, stop revenue leaks, and scale faster.
